# Kyoto & Osaka (Keihan) stays map, Nov 19-22 2026

Static map of every stay on jalan.net with a room for 2 adults on at least one night of Thu Nov 19 to Sun Nov 22 2026 (check-out Sunday):

- **Kyoto**: all of Kyoto city plus southern Kyoto (Jalan areas Kyoto Station, Kawaramachi/Karasuma, Gion/Higashiyama, Arashiyama, Ohara/Kurama, Uji/Nagaokakyo), any type (hotel, ryokan, guesthouse, hostel, apartment, machiya), any price.
- **Osaka**: stays within 1 km (straight line) of a Keihan station in central Osaka where Premium Car trains stop: Yodoyabashi, Kitahama, Temmabashi, Kyobashi.

Collected from jalan.net on 2026-11-01. Prices are per night for the room (2 adults), per plan and night.

- **Nights**: "All 3" keeps plans bookable for the whole stay (pins show the 3-night average per night); "Any" shows the cheapest single night, and pins of places that are not free on all three nights list the free nights (Th, Fr, Sa). Saturday Nov 21 is nearly sold out in Kyoto.
- **Keihan Premium Car station**: distance to the nearest station served by Premium Car trains (Demachiyanagi, Jingu-Marutamachi, Sanjo, Gion-Shijo, Kiyomizu-Gojo, Shichijo, Fushimi-Inari, Tambabashi, Chushojima in Kyoto; Yodoyabashi, Kitahama, Temmabashi, Kyobashi in Osaka; per keihan.co.jp). Walking times are estimates from the straight-line distance.
- Hover a pin for photos and the cheapest plans, click for all plans with booking links (whole stay or single nights), click a photo for the gallery. The URL hash keeps the view, open property and filters, so links can be shared.

Open `index.html` directly or via GitHub Pages. `data.js` is generated by `pipeline/` (Jalan scraping via browser-use, English summaries written by an LLM from the Jalan text; the raw data is not included here):
`discover.py` → `osaka_coords.py` → `scrape.py` (sharded) → `prep.py` → enrichment (`enrich/INSTRUCTIONS.md`) → `build_data.py`.

Prices and availability are a snapshot and change; check Jalan before booking. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors; photos and listing text © their owners on jalan.net.
